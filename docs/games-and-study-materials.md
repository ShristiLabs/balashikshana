# Interactive Study Materials & Game Engine Archetypes

Every unit in **Balashikshana** follows a structured **4-Stage Pedagogical Loop** that bridges conceptual understanding, guided practice, fluency automaticity, and mastery assessment.

```text
┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Stage 1: Study & Explore  │  Interactive Manipulatives + Voice-Narrated Micro-Stories
└─────────────┬─────────────┘
              ▼
┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Stage 2: Guided Practice  │  Untimed games with visual hints & step-by-step scaffolding
└─────────────┬─────────────┘
              ▼
┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Stage 3: Fluency Zone     │  Timed arcade mini-games targeting automatic recall speed
└─────────────┬─────────────┘
              ▼
┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Stage 4: Boss Gate Exam   │  Zero-hint stratified exam enforcing Accuracy % + Speed limit
└───────────────────────────┘
```

## 1. Interactive Study Materials (Stage 1)

Before playing timed games, children explore concepts hands-on using **Interactive Digital Manipulatives** and **Voice-Narrated Micro-Lessons**:

### 1.1 Mathematics Manipulatives
1. **Interactive 5-Frame, 10-Frame, Unifix Cube Trains & Domino Mats (`PK-M1` to `G1-M5`):**
   - Drag counters, seasonal objects (apples on a tree, buttons on a snowman, raindrops), linking cubes, or dominoes onto frames.
   - Highlights `5 + n` and `10 + n` structures, "+1" successive tower growth (`Grow & Shrink`), "Make-a-Ten" bridging (`8 + 6 = 8 + 2 + 4 = 10 + 4 = 14`), and "Decompose-to-Ten" subtraction (`13 - 4 = 13 - 3 - 1 = 9`).
2. **Base-10 Place Value Workbench & Place Value Flip Charts (`G1-M7` to `G3-M2`):**
   - Snap 10 unit cubes into a 10-rod, 10 rods into a 100-flat (`100` as a bundle of ten tens), and 10 flats into a 1,000-cube alongside interactive Place Value Flip Charts for expanded notation.
   - Interactive **Regrouping Crane** (bundles 10 ones into 1 ten for carrying), **Partial Sums / Break-Apart Splitter**, and **Unbundling Laser** (splits 1 hundred or 1 ten into 10 smaller units for borrowing, including borrowing across zeros like `500 - 267`).
3. **Bidirectional Number Line, Anchor Midpoints & Rounding Rollercoaster (`K-M2` to `G3-M7`):**
   - Zoomable from `0–5` up to `0–10,000`, with **anchor midpoint markers (`5, 10, 25, 50, 75`)**, empty number-line jump builders, and fractional partitions (`1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/6, 1/8`).
   - Visualizes skip-counting jumps, whole-number length sums/differences within 100, elapsed time intervals, and a hill curve for rounding to the nearest 10 or 100.
4. **Equal-Sign Balance Scale, Fact-Family Triangles & Variable Solver (`K-M2` to `G3-M6`):**
   - Physical balance scale that tips left/right for `<` and `>` or levels out for `=` (`True or Trash` equations like `5 + 2 = 2 + 5` and `8 + 4 = ? + 5`).
   - Interactive **Multiplication/Division and Addition/Subtraction Fact-Family Triangles** for unknown-addend (`10 - 8` via `8 + ? = 10`) and unknown-factor (`32 ÷ 8` via `8 × ? = 32`) reasoning.
5. **Array Slicer, Geoboard & Rectilinear Grid Blueprint (`G2-M6` to `G3-M9`):**
   - Drag rows and columns (`Circles & Stars`, rectangular arrays) to see multiplication (`6 × 8`), slice tiled rectangles into `(6 × 5) + (6 × 3)` to prove the **Distributive Property**, decompose L-shaped rectilinear rooms, and compare rectangles with the **same area but different perimeters** or **same perimeter but different areas**.
6. **Synchronized Fraction Bars, Paper Folding, Ruler Fractions & Pizza Cutter (`K-M9` to `G3-M8`):**
   - Cut circular/rectangular wholes into equal shares (including equal shares of identical wholes that have *different shapes*), fold virtual paper strips, and align fraction bars to prove equivalence (`1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6 = 4/8`), whole-number fractions (`3 = 3/1`, `4/4 = 1`), and valid comparisons referring to the **same whole**.
7. **Geared Judy Clock, Dual-Unit Rulers, Scaled Graphs & US Cash Register (`PK-M5` to `G3-M10`):**
   - Linked hour and minute hands with time-relationship charts (`60 sec = 1 min`, `60 min = 1 hr`, `24 hr = 1 day`, `AM/PM`).
   - End-to-end non-standard unit iteration (paperclips/cubes with no gaps or overlaps), indirect length comparison with a third benchmark object, dual measurement (`12 in = 1 ft`, `100 cm = 1 m`), and **quarter-inch (`1/4"`) / half-inch (`1/2"`) rulers** linked to fractional line plots and scaled pictographs/bar graphs.
   - High-resolution **two-sided (heads & tails) US coins** (Penny 1¢, Nickel 5¢, Dime 10¢, Quarter 25¢) and bills ($1, $5, $10, $20, $50, $100).

### 1.2 ELA / Reading Foundations & Grammar Manipulatives
1. **Continuous Phoneme Blending Slider & Elkonin Sound Boxes (`PK-E4` to `G1-E1`):**
   - Slide a finger across letter tiles (`c -> a -> t` or `sh -> i -> p`) with smooth continuous audio synthesis before snapping into the blended word.
2. **Magic-e Wand, Bossy-R & Vowel Team Sound Matrix (`G1-E3` to `G2-E3`):**
   - Interactive transformations (`cap -> cape`, `kit -> kite`) and mouth-position audio guides for diphthongs (`oi/oy, ou/ow`) and r-controlled vowels (`ar, er, ir, or, ur`).
3. **6-Syllable Type Splitter & Greek/Latin Root Tree (`G2-E4` to `G3-E7`):**
   - Slice multisyllabic words into Open, Closed, VCe, Vowel Team, R-Controlled, and Consonant-`-le` syllables; attach prefixes/suffixes to Greek/Latin roots (`tele, graph, port, spect`).
4. **Synchronized Leveled Anthology Reader (`K-E7` to `G3-E5`):**
   - Word-by-word audio highlighting for Read-Along texts, transitioning to independent Read-Alone passages with tap-to-define glossary popups and evidence highlighter tools.

### 1.3 Science & Social Studies Virtual Labs
1. **Phase Change & Thermal Chamber (`K-S1` to `G3-S1`):** Heat/cool solids, liquids, and gases to observe melting, freezing, evaporation, condensation, and particle motion.
2. **Force, Motion & Magnet Pole Bench (`K-S2` to `G3-S2`):** Test pushes, pulls, friction surfaces, balanced/unbalanced tug-of-war forces, and N/S magnetic attraction and repulsion.
3. **Life Cycle, Pedigree & Food Web Simulator (`K-S3` to `G3-S4`):** Construct butterfly/frog/flowering plant life cycles, trace inherited traits in pedigree charts, and balance producers/consumers in ecosystems.
4. **Interactive 50 US States, Continents & Supply/Demand Grapher (`K-SS2` to `G3-SS4`):** Explore cardinal directions, letter-number map grids, all 50 US states and capitals by region, and interactive supply-and-demand curves showing shortage and surplus.

## 2. The 10 Core Parameterized Game Archetypes (Stages 2 & 3)

All 130 units are powered by **10 modular, data-driven 2D game archetypes**. Each archetype loads unit-specific JSON question generators, sprites, and speed parameters:

| # | Archetype ID | Game Mechanics | Example Curriculum Skills & Units Powered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | `tactile-sorter` | Multi-bucket drag-and-drop or conveyor sorting with physics bounce feedback | • Classify shapes/colors/materials & Defining vs. Non-Defining attributes (`PK-M5`, `G1-M9`, `K-S1`)<br>• Consonant vs. Vowel / Short vs. Long Vowel (`K-E1`, `G1-E3`)<br>• Nouns/Verbs/Adjectives & Quadrilaterals (`PK-E6`, `G3-M9`) |
| 2 | `trace-draw-studio` | Guided Bezier stroke tracing with directional checkpoints and star trails | • Learn letters A–Z (`PK-E1`, `PK-E2`)<br>• Numeral writing 0–20 (`PK-M1`, `K-M1`) |
| 3 | `lilypad-jumper` | Number-line hopping character jumping by $+1, -1, +2, +5, +10, +100$, or fractions | • Counting forward from any number (`Number Squeeze`, `K-M2`, `K-M7`)<br>• Skip-counting by 2s, 5s, 10s, 100s (`G1-M1`, `G2-M1`)<br>• Ruler fractions on number lines & Elapsed time (`G3-M7`, `G3-M10`) |
| 4 | `block-builder` | Snap, bundle, and unbundle Ten-Frames, Unifix Cube Trains, and Base-10 Blocks | • Counting, Grow/Shrink towers & making 5 and 10 (`PK-M1`, `K-M3`, `K-M4`)<br>• Place Value, Partial Sums & 2/3-digit Regrouping (`G1-M7`, `G2-M3`, `G3-M2`) |
